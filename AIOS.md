# AIOS

This fork of [block/buzz](https://github.com/block/buzz) is the starting point
for **AIOS**, a commercial product built in Slovenia by
[@Skralo](https://github.com/Skralo).

- The fork syncs with block/buzz every day through
  [`.github/workflows/aios-sync-upstream.yml`](.github/workflows/aios-sync-upstream.yml).
- Buzz is licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE). AIOS keeps that
  license and its notices, and does not use the Buzz name or logo.
- The Buzz 1.0 desktop app lives in
  [block/buzz-app](https://github.com/block/buzz-app).
